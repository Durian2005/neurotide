NeuroTide includes the Simplified Chinese OCR model chi_sim.traineddata from tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast.

Source: https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
Model: fast integer LSTM trained data for Simplified Chinese (chi_sim)
License: Apache License 2.0

The model is processed by Tesseract.js and runs locally in the user's browser.
